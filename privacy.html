Privacy & playback

This website uses YouTube API Services to play videos. By using the YouTube player you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your listening experience

The YouTube player is loaded only when you choose to play. Track artwork is delivered by YouTube image servers, which receive your IP address and normal request information. Embedded playback connects to YouTube and Google and may use cookies or similar storage under their policies. No audio or video is downloaded or hosted by this website.

The visible player preserves YouTube's controls, branding, ads, and playback restrictions. Playback pauses when you leave the tab. Some browsers require a further click in the video to start playback and mobile volume may be controlled by your device.

Track requests

If you submit Create Your Track, your name or nickname, optional email and title, choices, instructions, and acceptance of the displayed terms are sent to the existing Google Form. Google processes the response and the project owner receives it in the linked private Sheet. This site does not read that Sheet and uses no Google account credentials. A successful iframe response does not let this site verify the private Sheet row. The form is operated by Google; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Local preferences

Saved track IDs and your volume setting stay in your browser's local storage. A short request cooldown timestamp stays in session storage. They are not sent to us. There are no accounts, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers added by this site. GitHub Pages and third-party providers may process connection logs under their own policies.

Fire Mix

Fire Mix is an experimental discovery playlist using approximately 30-second video segments from within each track through the official YouTube IFrame API. It starts only when selected. Buffering, keyframes, ads, and browser autoplay policies affect timing. There is no audio extraction, crossfading, ad skipping, or background playback. “Keep playing” exits discovery and continues the selected full video.

For questions about the project or catalog corrections, use the contact information available on the official channel.
